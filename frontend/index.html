Live rule warnings
Warnings are created from observed, closed five-second bars using explicit movement and volume thresholds. They are not XGBoost predictions or findings of market manipulation.
Rule-based · current session
ⓘ
Rules in this build: an absolute five-second close-to-close price move of at least 1.0%, or base volume at least 3× the median of the preceding 20 observed non-empty five-second bars. The volume rule stays in warm-up until its baseline exists. Scores are not probabilities; empty buckets are not invented.
